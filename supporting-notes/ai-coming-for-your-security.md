# Supporting Notes – AI Is Coming for Your Security

## Key Threat Scenarios
1. Employees using generative AI tools with sensitive business data
2. Prompt injection and model manipulation risks
3. AI-assisted phishing and social engineering attacks
4. Third-party AI integrations increasing supply chain exposure
5. Lack of visibility and auditability of AI-driven decisions

## Control Considerations
- Clear AI usage policies mapped to enforceable technical controls
- Data classification and input filtering for AI interactions
- AI vendor and third-party risk assessments
- Logging and monitoring of AI-related activity
- Incident response playbooks updated for AI-driven threats

These notes extend and support the published article and are intended for cyber security and governance professionals.
